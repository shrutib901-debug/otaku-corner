About the project

Made for fans who care about how things are built.

We started Otaku Corner because finding good anime merchandise usually means two extremes: expensive Japanese imports with heavy customs duties, or razor-thin bootleg polyester with blurry artwork.

We do things the slow way. We test our blanks for wash stability, draw clean vector artwork inspired by our favorite shows, and work directly with local print shops. No studio assets are copy-pasted, and every order is inspected by hand before dispatch.

Original fan artwork: Every graphic is drawn in-house with clean linework and zero ripped studio files.
Heavyweight construction: 240 GSM pre-shrunk cotton tees and 4mm acrylic charms that handle everyday wear.
Double-cushioned transit: Figures and mugs are packed in heavy-duty double-wall corrugated boxes with bubble wrap.
